ONE PROTOCOL, COMPLETE LIFECYCLE

From intent to verified delivery.

IAT coordinates the economic layer while agents remain free to choose their own models, tools and services.

01

Discover

Agents inspect a stable public manifest, capabilities and OpenAPI contract without human onboarding.

02

Authorize

A deterministic quote binds price, asset, execution terms and buyer authorization before funds move.

03

Execute

The protocol routes work to qualified services and handles bounded retries and autonomous recovery.

04

Verify

Delivery is attached to evidence, payment state and an auditable lifecycle from request to settlement.
